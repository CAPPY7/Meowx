MEOWX ICON PACK

indigo gradient with a cyan glow. Looks clean at 16 px and at 1024 px.

                      Meowx/Resources/Assets.xcassets/AppIcon.appiconset/

                      Icon Composer (Xcode 26) if you want the real Liquid
                      Glass icon: drag both in, background = bottom layer.
preview.png           how it looks light, dark, and tiny

Brand colors: violet #6A45FF, indigo #160E4A, cyan #22D3EE, magenta #E040FB,
white glyph #FFFFFF to #D8D0FF.
